Foundation · shared concepts

Foundation and process

This page gives the shared vocabulary, one process, and the links to every category. Read it before a topic page so the later terms have a place to attach.

30-second essentials
  • SLO is an internal reliability target; SLA is a contract.
  • SLI is the measurement behind a target.
  • An error budget is the allowed unreliability derived from the SLO.
  • The shared process is identify, measure, monitor, and respond.
01

Identify

Name the behavior, the user, and the scope.

02

Measure

Choose one metric and one target window.

03

Monitor

Review burn, alerts, and the reporting cadence.

04

Respond

Act on the threshold and record the decision.

01 · CONCEPTSShared definitions

TermDefinitionWhy it matters
SLOAn internal target for a reliability metric over a defined window.Sets the target without creating a customer contract.
SLIThe measurement used to evaluate the target.Makes the target observable.
SLAA contractual commitment with a consequence.Defines what happens when the commitment is missed.
Error budgetThe allowed unreliability derived from the SLO.Balances change speed with user trust.

02 · PARAMETERSWhat to fix before measuring

ParameterExampleRule
MetricSuccessful requests divided by valid requestsOne primary metric per SLO.
Window30 daysKeep the same window across comparisons.
Target99.9%State the target and the error budget together.

03 · MEASUREMENTOne formula to remember

Error budget(1 - SLO) x window

At 99.9% over 30 days, the error budget is 0.1% of the window. Read the number as an allowance, not a spending target.

04 · MONITORINGWhat to review

  • Burn rate: how fast the budget is consumed against the target.
  • Alerts: who is notified, at what threshold, and with what action.
  • Drift: a metric that no longer represents the user-visible behavior.

Exam traps
  • SLO and SLA are not synonyms.
  • An error budget is not a cost limit.
  • A target without a measurement is only a wish.

06 · SELF-CHECKAnswer without looking

What is the difference between an SLO and an SLA?

An SLO is an internal target. An SLA is a contractual commitment with a consequence, such as a service credit.

Why does the error budget matter?

It turns reliability into an allowance. Teams can spend it on change or slow down when it is exhausted.
