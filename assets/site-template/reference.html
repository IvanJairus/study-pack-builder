02 · GLOSSARYTerms in plain language

⌕
SLO
An internal target for a reliability metric over a defined window.
SLI
The measurement used to evaluate an SLO, such as successful requests divided by valid requests.
Error budget
The allowed amount of unreliability derived from the SLO.
RTO
The maximum acceptable time to restore a service after an incident.
RPO
The maximum acceptable data loss, measured as time.
