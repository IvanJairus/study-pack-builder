01 · CONCEPTSFour terms that are often mixed up

TermMeaningExample
SLOInternal reliability target.99.9% of requests succeed over 30 days.
SLIThe measurement behind the target.Successful requests divided by valid requests.
SLAContractual commitment with a consequence.Service credits if availability drops below 99.5%.
Error budgetAllowed unreliability from the SLO.0.1% of the 30-day window.
Exam traps
  • Do not treat SLO and SLA as synonyms. SLO is internal; SLA is contractual.
  • MTTR measures recovery speed, not failure frequency. MTBF measures the time between failures.
  • RTO is time to restore. RPO is acceptable data loss, measured in time.

02 · SELF-CHECKAnswer without looking

Why is an error budget useful?

It turns reliability into a budget. Teams can spend it on change, or slow down when the budget is exhausted. It balances delivery speed with user trust.

What is the difference between RTO and RPO?

RTO is the maximum acceptable recovery time. RPO is the maximum acceptable amount of data loss, expressed as time. A short RPO requires frequent backups or replication.
