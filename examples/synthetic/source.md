# Synthetic Source: Reliability Basics

## Section 1: Service targets

A Service Level Objective (SLO) is an internal target for a reliability metric over a defined window. A Service Level Indicator (SLI) is the measurement used to evaluate the target. A Service Level Agreement (SLA) is a contractual commitment with a consequence, such as a service credit.

An error budget is the allowed amount of unreliability derived from the SLO. If the SLO is 99.9 percent over 30 days, the error budget is 0.1 percent of that window.

## Section 2: Recovery

Recovery Time Objective (RTO) is the maximum acceptable time to restore a service. Recovery Point Objective (RPO) is the maximum acceptable data loss, measured in time.

Mean Time To Recovery (MTTR) measures how quickly a service is restored after an incident. Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF) measures the time between failures.
